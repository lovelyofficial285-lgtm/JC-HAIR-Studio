Meet Jaydee Azure

Jaydee is the heart and hands behind JC Hair Studio. With an obsession for beautiful blonding and flawless, seamless tape-in extensions, she believes great hair should feel as healthy as it looks.

Every appointment begins with a relaxed, personalised consultation — colour mapping, extension matching and a plan tailored to your hair, your lifestyle and your goals. No rush, no guesswork, just honest advice and hair you'll love long after you leave the chair.
